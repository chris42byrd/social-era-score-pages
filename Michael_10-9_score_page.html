The main gap occurred at the end during the close; due to the prospect's distraction with the trucker, Christopher rushed the final numbers instead of anchoring them to the pain. When a prospect gets physically distracted during the pricing reveal, pause and ask if it's better to reschedule the final 5 minutes rather than rushing and letting the prospect dictate the 'send me an email' next step.

What Went Well

  • Setting the frame of the call immediately and respectfully dismantling the prospect's expectation of a 'pay per appointment' model before diving in.
  • Uncovering exact business metrics (close rates, margins, ad spend, CPL increases) to build a personalized ROI case.
  • Perfectly aligning the product's mechanics (the required budget filter on the landing page) to the prospect's biggest pain point (getting leads for $100 gate repairs).

What to Do Differently

  • When a prospect gets physically distracted during the pricing reveal (e.g., the trucker incident), pause and ask if they need 5 minutes to handle it or if you should reschedule, rather than rushing the numbers.
  • Explicitly ask 'Where do we go from here?' after the pitch instead of letting the prospect take control of the close by asking you to email them.
  • Dig deeper into the true cost of inaction by asking stark questions like: 'If you continue spending $10k a month on Yelp without hiring a sales team, where is the business 12 months from now?'
